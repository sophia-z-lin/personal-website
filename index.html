When does an evaluation deserve to be trusted?

NIST2026–present

Characterizing LLM Judges as Measurement Instruments

I led the design of a measurement framework testing entailment, claim reasoning and decomposition, error recognition, confidence, and abstention. More than 15 probes compare blind, correct-guidance, and deliberately false-guidance conditions to separate evaluation capability from prompt sensitivity and sycophancy.

Project lead
NISTOngoing

Adversarial Verification for Agentic AI Grounding

I am designing controlled experiments to test whether independent verifier agents with competing objectives improve factual grounding. The evaluation tooling tests citation-grounded reasoning, deception, hallucination, contradiction handling, and reliance on retrieved evidence when it conflicts with parametric knowledge.
