What's the point of this required post?

Some of my blog pieces are weird. I think it's polite to give you a heads-up.

Why write on so many different topics?

'Cause we are living in an interdisciplinary world / And I am an interdisciplinary girl.

Are any of these works published elsewhere?

Yes! Previously published work is archived here and clearly labeled.

I'm trying to find your opinions on ___. Why don't you tell me which pieces to take seriously?

While I don't think all my posts are equally important — that judgment is largely yours — I do think they deserve to be approached with the same respect.

Does writing something here mean you believe it? Are these beliefs or hypotheses? Can I cite this against you in five years?

I think of my selves at different moments as distinct people. Becoming someone new entails a kind of ego death: the new person destroys the previous version simply by existing. They inherit responsibility for their predecessor's actions, but do not necessarily inherit their beliefs. (Huh, maybe I should write an essay about Al Ewing…) I (currently) think almost all my opinions are mutable hypotheses. Some are much less likely to change than others (that people should be treated with dignity, for example), but I've completely reversed several opinions over the years. The material I post often contains beliefs that I, at the moment of publishing, hold incredibly tentatively but nevertheless think offer useful perspectives to think through.

Yes, you can hold future me accountable for what present me does. You'll have to ask her what she thinks, though — I suspect she thinks a lot of my takes are dead wrong!

What happens if I'm convinced you're wrong?

Feel free to publish a response, but I'd love to hear from you directly, too! If you change my mind, I'll put that on the blog.
